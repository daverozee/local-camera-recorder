MAKE ROOM FOR WHAT MATTERS

Storage settings

Clips are organized in a LocalCam subfolder by camera and date. Existing recordings stay in their original location if you change this path.

Original mode splits at camera keyframes, so clips may be slightly longer. Recording pauses when free space drops below the reserve.

When enabled, LocalCam deletes its indexed clips older than this limit, including clips in previous recording folders. Export anything you want to keep outside the archive.

Stop all cameras before changing these settings.
FIND YOUR CAMERAS

Network discovery

Scans every address, checks common camera ports, listens for ONVIF announcements, and retries nearby devices. Confirmed Foscam streams are added automatically. Existing credentials and names are preserved.
